About This Agreement

Purpose & General Considerations

The Placement Site will offer supervised learning experiences for Interns, including internships, clinical placements, cooperative education, practicum, service learning, capstone projects, work-study assignments, or other approved activities by OCC. It must be an established physical location, not be a home-based or cottage-industry work site unless written approval is obtained from OCC.

The OCC Contact and Placement Site Contact listed in the Contract Information section will be the primary representatives for this Agreement. Either party may replace a representative by written notice.

However, Interns actively in a learning experience at termination may complete their placement. Responsibilities of OCC

OCC shall:

  1. Provide course information and objectives, and ensure Interns meet academic and program requirements consistent with OCC’s program.
  2. Inform Interns of applicable Placement Site policies, professional conduct expectations, and responsibilities under this Agreement.
  3. Retain responsibility for academic instruction, evaluation, grading, and the awarding of academic credit.
  4. Provide oversight, maintain communication with the Placement Site and Interns, monitor Intern progress, and help resolve any issues during the placement.
  5. Require interns to dress professionally following the standards set by the Placement Site.
  6. Maintain the confidentiality of Placement Site information and comply with privacy laws.
  7. Maintain required insurance coverage as outlined in the Insurance section.

Responsibilities of Placement Site

The Placement Site shall:

  1. Provide supervised learning that aligns with OCC program goals, and orient Interns to Placement Site policies, procedures, and expectations.
  2. Assign a supervisor to oversee Intern activities and communicate with OCC about Intern progress and any concerns.
  3. Ensure a safe work environment with necessary equipment and resources, and inform about workplace risks.
  4. Comply with applicable laws regarding nondiscrimination, harassment, labor standards, and internship requirements.
  5. Unless otherwise agreed, Interns will be regarded as trainees without any expectation of receiving compensation.
  6. For paid internships, compensate Interns in accordance with applicable laws and agreed terms.
  7. Ensure confidentiality of student education records and personal information, comply with FERPA, and limit disclosure as required by law.
  8. Cooperate with OCC on Intern investigations and resolve concerns informally when appropriate.
  9. Maintain required insurance coverage as outlined in the Insurance section.

Insurance Requirements

Each party shall maintain insurance coverage that is both legally required and suitable for its role under this Agreement. Upon reasonable request, each party must provide certificates of insurance or other acceptable evidence of coverage to demonstrate compliance with these requirements.

  1. Common Insurance Requirements (OCC and Placement Site):

    Each party shall obtain and maintain:

    • Commercial General Liability (CGL) insurance, and if necessary, Commercial Umbrella Liability insurance, with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence. Such coverage shall be written on an occurrence basis and shall include premises and operations liability, products-completed operations liability, personal and advertising injury, and contractual liability (including liability assumed under an insured contract).
    • Workers’ Compensation and Employers’ Liability Insurance as required by Michigan law.
  2. Additional Requirements for Placement Site:

    In addition to the coverage listed above, the Placement Site shall maintain:

    • Professional Liability Insurance

      Professional errors and omissions liability insurance appropriate to its profession with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per loss, covering professional errors, acts, or omissions arising from activities and services performed under this Agreement.

Indemnification

To the extent permitted by law, claims, actions, causes of action, costs, expenses, and losses (including attorneys’ fees) arising out of or resulting from the acts or omissions of either party, or their respective employees, students, or agents shall be governed by applicable statutory and common law principles of indemnification, contribution, and equitable restitution.

  1. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Michigan.
  2. This Agreement is the complete understanding between parties and can only be amended in writing by both.
  3. If any part of this Agreement is found invalid or unenforceable, the rest will remain effective. Not enforcing a provision does not waive that provision or any other rights.
  4. Neither is liable for delays or failures due to events beyond their control, such as natural disasters, war, labor disruptions, or similar force majeure events as long as they promptly notify the other party.
