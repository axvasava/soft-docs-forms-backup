Mileage

Note to Originator: If available, select the From and To locations using the dropdown menus below.

If the desired location does not appear in either dropdown, manually enter it. In some cases, standard mileage is automatically calculated based on your entries. If no mileage is returned, enter the mileage manually.

Selecting Round Trip doubles the dollar amount for that row. No calculation is attempted until the Date, From, and To inputs are filled.

Reimbursable Mileage: Business mileage traveled on normal workdays is reimbursed only for mileage beyond the traveler’s normal commute.

Reimbursable mileage per day = total actual mileage driven minus normal work commute mileage.
