Authorization Form

Employee/Dependent Tuition

Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required.

Employees: Employees initiating their tuition waiver for classes pursuant to OCC Administrative Policies 2.3.3 or a collective bargaining agreement must complete Section I and submit the form to Human Resources.
Dependents / Spouses: Dependents and spouses applying for a tuition waiver must submit this form to Enrollment Services each semester.
Faculty: Full-time and retired faculty members and their spouses must pay for classes and apply for tuition reimbursement at the end of the semester.
Adjunct Faculty: Adjunct faculty members, spouses, and dependents must pay for classes and apply for tuition reimbursement at the end of the semester.

Employee Information

Faculty members and spouses and retired faculty members and spouses shall be granted tuition reimbursement upon completion of a course. Faculty members shall not be compensated for their spouses or dependent children enrolled in their own sections.
