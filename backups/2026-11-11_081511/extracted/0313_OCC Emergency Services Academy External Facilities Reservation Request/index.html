Proper use of Oakland Community College (OCC) facilities will be the responsibility of the sponsoring organization/renter to comply with state and federal laws, and local laws governing the use of public property and OCC’s Policy Guidelines for Freedom of Speech and Expression. To accommodate equal and fair access to college facilities, OCC requires rental organization to comply with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and all other applicable federal, state, and local regulations. Rental organization shall notify OCC of any specific accommodations that are requested of it by its participants, spectators, or any other individual relating to accessibility. All Firefighter related training will adhere to the appropriate National Fire Protection Agency (NEPA) standards. All other training will adhere to applicable industry safety standards. Instructional personnel are to be certified in the appropriate discipline. It is agreed that the sponsoring organization/renter will pay all rental fees and expenses as well as any repair fees resulting from use of this facility. All payments are due within 30 days of invoice. The Soliciting and Sale of Merchandise is Strictly Prohibited.
