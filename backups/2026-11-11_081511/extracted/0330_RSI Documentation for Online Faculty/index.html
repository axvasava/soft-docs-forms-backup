RSI Documentation for Online Faculty

Regular and Substantive Interaction is defined by the HLC as engaging students in teaching, learning, and assessment consistent with the content under discussion in the following ways:
  1. Providing direct instruction;
  2. Assessing or providing feedback on a student's coursework;
  3. Providing information or responding to questions about the content of a course or competency;
  4. Facilitating a group discussion regarding the content of a course or competency; or
  5. Other instructional activities approved by HLC or the program's accrediting agency.
To meet this requirement, faculty at OCC will engage in RSI in all online courses on a weekly basis for 12-15 week courses, and twice a week in compressed courses, using two or more of these strategies over the course of the term. Please use the form below to document your RSI plan for each online course that you teach. You should complete a separate form for each unique course you teach. Further information about RSI is available here. If you have additional questions, please reach out to your dean.
