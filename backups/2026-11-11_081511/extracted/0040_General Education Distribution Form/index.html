Must allow a minimum of 5 semester days (Fall & Winter) to respond and more than 50% of the total ballot responses are required for it to be a valid ballot. Voting Options are:
  • Aye (Yes)
  • Nay (No)
  • Abstain (non-vote that counts as a response in determining if it's a valid ballot)
  • No Response (ballot was sent but ignored, this does not count as a response in determining if it's a valid ballot)
