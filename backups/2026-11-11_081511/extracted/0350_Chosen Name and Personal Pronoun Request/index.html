Please Read and Complete the Following Information

  • OCC recognizes that students employees students and employees may use a first name other than their legal first name to identify themselves. As long as the use of this chosen name is not for the purpose of misrepresentation, OCC acknowledges that a chosen first name can be used where possible in the course of OCC business and education. OCC reserves the right to deny or remove, with or without notice, any chosen name for misuse, including but not limited to fraud, misrepresentation, attempting to avoid legal obligation, or the use of highly offensive or derogatory names. Students' last names (surnames) will remain unchanged. Employees, including student-employees, may request a chosen last name below.
  • Please be advised that requesting a chosen first name is not the same as legally changing your name through the court system. Selecting a chosen name does not change your legal name on OCC's records. If you have a legal name change, please provide supporting legal documentation and complete the Student or Employee Update Information Request form.
  • Use of a chosen personal pronoun also provides one way for expression, and requestors have the option to choose a pronoun below.
  • Chosen first name and chosen personal pronoun will be used on class rosters, grade rosters, and student schedules employee schedules student and employee schedules.
  • Legal name will be used on certain records, including but not limited to official transcripts, diplomas, employment forms, payroll records, tax documents, financial aid documents and records, verification requests, third-party reports such as to the National Student Clearinghouse, and other records that require use of a legal name on record.
  • To verify identity of the requestor, a valid OCC ID or government ID is required with this form for changes to be processed unless it is sent from your OCC student email account OCC employee email account OCC student or employee email account.
